Programs

Data Monetization A de-identified dataset built on unmodified, native NCPDP transmissions captured at the point of dispense, as the pharmacy sent them. That is what cannot be recreated: datasets assembled downstream from switches or payer adjudication are already modified and never see cash, discount-card, rejected, or reversed activity, and the source is an installed pharmacy base a competitor would have to own the software to reach. Serves patient-journey and real-world evidence platforms, market access and payer intelligence, life-sciences market and competitive intelligence, prescriber-level commercial analytics, financial and alt-data research, and community-pharmacy clinical networks. Tokenization is what unlocks it, letting customers stitch a patient journey across pharmacy systems and link it to the medical claims and lab data they already hold.
Copay Assistance & Voucher Exists to stop patients from abandoning a prescription at the counter because the copay is too high. The pharmacy keeps the fill and the revenue instead of returning it to stock; the manufacturer gets a patient who starts and stays on therapy, plus the claim-level visibility to prove the program worked; we bill on the program activity we process and deliver the reporting back that the manufacturer measures performance and satisfies contractual audit obligations with. I own the engineering and the delivery commitments, not the program.
Clinical Exists to change a filling behaviour at the counter: a lapsed patient filling again, a 30-day patient moved to 90-day, a formulary switch the plan now prefers. We find those patients in our own transaction data, turn each into a care goal, and put it in front of pharmacy staff inside the dispensing software, which is the only place a patient who is not filling can reliably be reached. The pharmacy earns on each goal its staff engages, the manufacturer gets the behaviour changed plus the outcome reporting that wins the renewal, and we bill on engagement and report on outcome. Same boundary: engineering and delivery commitments owned, program direction owned by the program team.
